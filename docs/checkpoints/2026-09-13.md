## Completed display change

Branch: `feat/inspector-snapshot-expiry`. Focus: truthful held inspection, not automatic polling or new controls.

The old browser kept its initial fresh/verified badges and enabled buttons indefinitely after one GET, even though server snapshots age out at 20 seconds. The patch adds a visible age/last-known card, expires the selected source in place, disables controls, and checks age again immediately before a POST. The server still enforces exact owner/revision checks; the finding does not demonstrate a wrong-owner mutation bypass.

Server-relative RPC age avoids browser/server clock skew. Unknown age fails closed. Request duration and browser elapsed time count toward expiry; switching the selected session does not reset them. Refresh is explicit, preserves exact selection and existing missing-owner behavior, and never auto-replays a command. Work/process/task text is retained as historical observation. Credential-specific redaction, inert text rendering, family separation, smaller eye and blue motes are unchanged.

Previews: [landscape](../snapshot-expiry-landscape-2026-09-13.png), [compact](../snapshot-expiry-compact-2026-09-13.png). Both are actual synthetic browser screenshots and were visually inspected. The compact view scrolls; its close button stays accessible. No production data was used.
