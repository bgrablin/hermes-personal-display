What each card is verifying
  • quiet watch: gaze centered, brows skeptical-neutral. Verifies the puppet does not always look right.
  • look left and look right: plain non-side-eye gaze cards, so reviewer can compare center vs left vs right directly.
  • side-eye left and side-eye right: must read as distinctly different stills, not just label changes.
  • reasoning: focused calm squint, neutral mouth, scan beam visible, not sad/distressed.
  • planning and working: bounded active states with restrained float/wing knobs and gold active accent.
  • warm load: adaptive CPU/load throttling slows blink, float, wing and frame budgets without making the character look broken.
  • completed: smug arch brow, asymmetric smirk, gaze off-right.
  • blocked: patient serious side-eye, not panic.
  • feed stale: calm diagnostic look toward telemetry without incident drama.
  • night watch: heavy lids, sleepy mouth, dim aura, gaze down.
  • critical: red/serious preset reserved for true local issues.
